# ADR 0010 — Legal department (37) architecture (`services/legal-py`)

- **Status:** accepted for build (Sep 26, 2026). Built, tested and live-run; **not in force for any real
  document, acceptance or counsel answer** (see "Unlock list").
- **Spec:** `LEGAL_SPEC.md` rev 1 (binding), with `reports/Legal department world class model.md` (LR) for deferred
  detail, BUILD_CONTRACTS.md, and the existing Legal port contracts in compliance-py, clipper-network-py,
  creative-py, verification-py and onboarding-py.
- **Base:** branch `legal-department` from `integration-2026-09-24` @ `b612ed2`.
- **Nothing in this service, this ADR or its outputs is legal advice.**

## Context

Legal is the department whose value is proof that a licensed lawyer decided (LR). It keeps the document register
and acceptance evidence, runs playbooks, tracks obligations and filings, triages matters and holds, runs the
takedown desk, applies the music policy and records counsel answers. It never enforces (Compliance 38), pays
(Finance 31) or executes rights clearance (Creative). Founder-locked (§0.1): no legal advice; only Andre approves,
and for documents and playbook positions only after a counsel sign-off record exists; a counsel memo is the only
thing that turns an unverified item verified; no LLM calls; uploaded text is data.

## Decision

A new FastAPI + pydantic v2 service with the house conventions of compliance-py and verification-py (the
post-AEGIS-round-16 / fix-wave-17 versions): hardened uvicorn launcher (`serve.py`), refuse-to-start config,
service bearer + caller tokens (`X-LEGAL-Caller-Token`) + Andre's FounderGate (`X-Andre-Approval-Token`),
per-route body limits, docs off, 127.0.0.1 by default (port 8420), strict models, `request_id` idempotency
(15 min, 409 on a different body), record-first on the evidence ledger (department `legal`, ids
`lg-<abbrev>-<40 hex>`), a hash-chained local log anchored line-by-line on the ledger with the instance lease and
Andre's reconcile (identical procedure to verification-py, including N16-7), and thin clients with a 10 s total
wall-clock deadline.

## Numbered choices where the spec is silent (safest option taken)

1. **All seven seed files are pinned**, not only the rules seed. Only the rules seed has the N14-13 non-production
   override (`LEGAL_ALLOW_UNPINNED_SEED=1` + `LEGAL_SEED_SHA256`; `/health` then says `rules_pinned: false`); any
   other changed seed refuses start with no override.
2. **Blob store.** Every text (document texts, template-fill variables, memos, quoted excerpts, counsel questions
   and proposed edits, clause texts, counterparty paper, acceptance evidence, e-sign certificates, rendered outbound
   notices) is a blob `LEGAL_DATA_DIR/blobs/<sha256>`, written before the log line that cites it; a torn `.tmp` is
   removed at start; a file whose name is not its SHA-256, or a cited blob that is missing (and not recorded
   deleted), refuses start (G5).
3. **No text in the log or on the ledger.** Log records keep hashes, ids, enums, dates; ledger payloads keep ids,
   hashes, enums, dates, integers, rule and cq ids. The Andre-typed memo excerpt is sent to Compliance (the
   proposal evidence needs it) and nowhere else. Caller-supplied refs that could identify people
   (`requester_ref`, `signer_identity_ref`) are hashed in ledger payloads.
4. **Legal's calendar dates are Los Angeles dates** (`LEGAL_BUSINESS_TZ`, default `America/Los_Angeles`): receipt
   dates, due dates, filing windows, DSAR clocks. Job idempotency uses the UTC date (spec §E).
5. **Business day** = Mon-Fri minus the observed US federal holidays (5 U.S.C. 6103 list with the Saturday->Friday,
   Sunday->Monday shift) seeded for 2026-2030. A count that would leave the seeded range is refused
   `HOLIDAYS_UNKNOWN` (never guessed). A receipt on a weekend or holiday counts from that calendar date (the next
   business day is day 1). Counsel confirms the definition before first use.
6. **`counsel_required`** is true for every §D row except `sow`, `soi_*` and `fbn_*` ("template only", "approve
   once", "countersigns" and "review before filing" all read as counsel-required).
7. **Doc types.** `not_legal_advice_v1` uses a new `routing_notice` type (the §B.1 enum has none); `tm_zbm`/`tm_zbc`
   are `data_sheet`.
8. **Data sheets** `soi_<entity>` / `fbn_<dba>` are created by Andre's first upload (counsel_required false).
9. **Entity.** Every version names its entity, which must be one of the document's entities (ZBC and ZBM are
   separate); a fill keeps the template's entity.
10. **Versions** are immutable and strictly increasing (`major.minor`). After `review_by` a document is not
    current until a new version (possibly the same text, so the same hash) gets a fresh sign-off and approval.
11. **Template fills.** Placeholders `{{name}}` are replaced by typed variables (string <= 500 printable single-line
    characters, date, int, enum); the template is the highest in-force approved version that declares variables
    and is not itself a fill. A fill is a new version with its own SHA-256 and, when `counsel_required`, needs its
    own counsel sign-off on that hash (strict reading of LG-02); it carries `review_label:
    counsel_template:<doc>@<v>` meanwhile.
12. **Counsel sign-off record.** For documents: a filed memo from the same `counsel_ref`, whose SHA-256 matches and
    whose `cites.doc_versions` names `doc_id@version`, plus `doc_sha256` equal to the version's hash. For the
    engagement letter (no memo can exist before an engagement): counsel's countersigned copy, stored as a blob; its
    `counsel_ref` becomes the engagement id. Renewal under the same `counsel_ref` is a new version.
13. **LG-17.** A memo is accepted only when its `counsel_ref` names the engagement whose letter version is the
    *current* engagement letter; a lapsed letter stops memo intake until re-approved.
14. **Counsel questions that block approvals (LG-18):** `ic_agreement` needs CQ-17 verified;
    `not_legal_advice_v1` needs CQ-15; `engagement_letter` needs clause `ENG-AI-01` in its clause list (CQ-24
    blocks it "without the AI clauses").
15. **Acceptances.** The version must be approved and in force at `accepted_at` (spec wording; not necessarily
    the highest version, so concurrent per-client instances work). `evidence_sufficient` is computed once at record
    time and never changes afterwards (a later CQ-19 memo helps new acceptances only, S4).
16. **Forbidden keys.** IP, user-agent, device, DOB, government-id and payment keys are refused (422) anywhere in
    any request body, before parsing, and no model has such a field (G6).
17. **Clause matching** is SHA-256 equality of normalized text (NFKC, case-fold, collapsed whitespace); anything else
    is `unmatched`. A repeated clause id: the first position counts. A walk-away fact that is missing or
    `unknown` escalates. A playbook change needs a filed memo citing **every** clause id of the proposed playbook;
    versions increase; the base version is re-checked at approval. Weakening flags: `clause_removed`,
    `standard_changed`, `fallback_added_or_changed`, `walk_away_removed`, `escalation_loosened`,
    `obligations_removed`, `lead_time_shortened` (approval needs `acknowledge_weakening`).
18. **`LEGAL_AGENT_MAX_FALLBACK`** accepts 0 or 1 only; the playbook schema refuses any escalation other than
    `counsel` for `fallback_2` and `unmatched`.
19. **Counterparty paper** = a review without `our_template_version`, with uploaded paper text, or naming a version
    that is not an approved version of that document type: the whole document is `unmatched` -> counsel.
20. **Obligations.** Due fields are the executed version's date variables plus `accepted_at` and `effective_at`
    (LA dates); `Nbd` offsets use the business calendar. An unbindable due date creates the obligation with
    `due: null, unbound: true` (never dropped). `done` only from the owner department's caller token
    (`legal_37`/`andre` owners need Andre's token). Counterparty-paper obligations are entered through
    `POST /legal/v1/obligations` (Andre + a memo citing that paper's `doc@version`) — a route the spec text implies
    but its table omits.
21. **A third money string.** `disputed_amount_usd` (BUILD_CONTRACTS §1 format) on intake, needed to compare a
    contract dispute with `LEGAL_DISPUTE_THRESHOLD` (§E lists two money fields; S4 cannot be decided without an
    amount). A missing amount is ambiguous -> escalate + hold.
22. **Triage details.** Likelihood counts true facts among `counterparty_represented`, `deadline_stated`,
    `prior_dispute` (an unknown counts as true); a dispute below the threshold is S6 (playbook lane); an internal
    `filing_lapsed` matter is S6 -> counsel_standard, no hold.
23. **Holds.** Default systems when none are named: email, chat, drive, legal_store. `legal_store` is frozen by
    Legal itself (the deletion check); Cybersecurity-owned systems are `not_frozen` behind the stand-in and Andre
    is alerted (push stand-in). The notice is sent only from an approved `lit_hold_notice` template to named
    custodians (People 43 stand-in: not sent, alert). A matter with an active hold cannot close. Release needs
    Andre's token and a filed memo (the memo `cites` structure has no matter field, so any filed memo is accepted).
24. **Takedowns.** A valid `platform_post` notice opens an `ip_claim` matter with `deadline_stated: true` (S3/L2) so a
    hold on `post:<sha>` issues (preserve first). `zbc_hosted`: removal is forwarded (Legal removes nothing) and a
    per-`uploader_ref` repeat count is returned; applying a §512(i) policy is not built. Added routes `restore` and
    `withdraw` (the spec's restore refusal and count exclusions need them). The counter-notice checklist is stored
    as a hash and labelled UNVERIFIED. A restore after the 14th business day is allowed with `RESTORE_LATE`. A
    claimant action opens an S2 matter with a hold and blocks restore.
25. **Outbound notices** render from the approved `dmca_procedure` template; with `license_or_fair_use_possible:
    true` a filed counsel memo id is required (else 409 + a counsel package). Status is `ready_not_delivered`: no
    delivery channel exists; `outbound_notice_sent` records Andre's signature and the rendered hash.
26. **Filings.** §8/§15 window = registration + 5..6 years; §9 = + 9..10 years (opening UNVERIFIED); SOS window from
    `formation_month` + `due_year`; 29 February -> 28 February; a trademark filing is marked `ready` only with CQ-27
    verified; a lapse opens a matter and alerts Andre. Fees are reference strings, never money fields.
27. **Music.** All rules evaluated (complete reasons); CQ-21 is required for any commercial-library use (paid or
    not); "absent" music with a source or track id is `MUSIC_SOURCE_UNDECLARED`; the verified library list is the
    LG-12 parameter (`tiktok`).
28. **Sign-off topics.** The memo body carries Andre-typed scopes `{<scope_key>: [ids]}` or `{"any": true}` for cited
    topics; facts must be exactly `{<scope_key>: [ids]}` inside the scope; `reference` = the memo id; review_by =
    memo date + 90 days. Unknown topics are recorded as `unknown` (never the caller's text).
29. **Memo intake.** Aliases are refused (cite the canonical row). Rows owned by Compliance (CQ-01..14) stay
    `unverified` in Legal's mirror until Compliance reports the replacement row `verified` (the daily
    proposal-delivery job reads `GET /compliance/v1/register/{replacement}`); Legal-, V&I-, CN- and Finance-origin
    rows turn verified at intake and their owners are told through (stand-in) ports. `review_by` = memo date +
    `LEGAL_MEMO_REVIEW_DAYS` (<= 90). A memo date more than a day ahead is refused; a duplicate memo (SHA-256) is
    refused. The Compliance `request_id` is derived from memo and target, so a retry is Compliance-idempotent. A 4xx
    from Compliance -> `refused_by_compliance` (not retried); transport, 5xx, redirect or deadline ->
    `pending_delivery` (retried). The spec's proposal `note` is not sent: Compliance's strict `ProposalRequest` has no
    such field (it would be a 422).
30. **Invalidate** (Compliance only tightens) accepts a cq id (an alias invalidates its canonical row),
    `retention:<class>` and `signoff:<topic>`.
31. **Retention** deletes blobs only (the log is append-only). Periods `P<n>Y` = 365n days, `M` = 30n, `D` = n. A blob
    is deleted only when its class is verified and unexpired, its period has elapsed and no active hold lists any of
    its subject refs; the deletion is recorded before the file goes. A blob shared by identical content keeps the
    subject refs of its first writer.
32. **Jobs** are idempotent per (job, UTC date); the proposal-delivery job makes its remote calls outside the lock.
33. **Refusals** are recorded best-effort (an event and a local line); a refusal stands whether or not it was
    recorded (`recorded` says which). `RULES_NOT_IN_FORCE` refusals are not recorded.
34. **Idempotent answers persist** through the log, so a replay after a restart (within 15 minutes) returns the
    stored answer.
35. **Dual routes.** Where Andre or a caller may call, a present `X-Andre-Approval-Token` is verified (a wrong one is
    403 and recorded; it never falls back to a caller token). "Any caller" reads accept any caller token or
    Andre's token.
36. **Labels (§A.2).** Outputs carry `review_label`: `unreviewed`, `counsel_memo:<id>`,
    `counsel_template:<doc>@<v>` or `counsel_countersigned:<ref>`; triage and playbook outputs also carry
    `unreviewed: true`.
37. **Advice guard.** 14 patterns (the four in §A.3 first). Normalization: HTML entities, NFKC, format characters
    removed and as spaces, Cyrillic/Greek confusables, case-fold, apostrophe variants and contractions, punctuation
    to spaces; views with and without leetspeak folding; runs of three or more single characters re-joined against
    space-free patterns. Applied to template-fill variables, outbound-notice variables and every Legal-generated
    reason message (at construction). Counsel-authored bodies and counsel-bound packages are not scanned for advice
    (they are counsel's or go to counsel); they are scanned for injection.
38. **Extra read routes:** `GET /legal/v1/integrity`, `/intelligences`, `/playbooks/{doc_type}`,
    `/takedowns/{id}`, `/documents/{id}/versions/{v}/text` (Andre only); extra writes `POST /filings/{id}/ready`,
    `/takedowns/{id}/restore`, `/takedowns/{id}/withdraw`, `POST /obligations`.
39. **Contract terms.** Stored values come from the caller except `signed`, `signed_at` and
    `ccpa_cpra_clause_present`, which are recomputed at every read from the acceptance and the executed version;
    a `put` whose `signed` or `ccpa_cpra_clause_present` differs from the evidence is 409; the acceptance must be the
    client's own (`client:<id>`) and of `client_msa`.
40. **Refused configurations:** `LEGAL_COUNSEL_CHANNEL` / `LEGAL_ESIGN_PROVIDER` set (not built),
    `LEGAL_PORTAL_FAQ=1`, `LEGAL_MEMO_REVIEW_DAYS` or `LEGAL_HOLD_RENOTICE_DAYS` above 90, a partial
    `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_*` set, an unknown time zone, a caller token equal to the service or Andre token.

## Routes

As spec §E, plus the extras in choice 38. Protocol endpoints (§E.1): `GET /documents/{id}/current` (compliance-py
`Legal37Port`, clipper-network-py `LegalPort`), `POST /signoffs` (creative-py), `GET /takedowns/count`
(verification-py), `GET|PUT /contracts/{client_id}/terms` (onboarding-py). `tests/contract_maps.py` applies each
caller's documented mapping to Legal's real answers using the callers' own answer dataclasses.

## Spec items not built, and why

- **"Changes other services must make" (1-7)** — reported, not made (brief). No service calls Legal yet.
- **A real counsel channel, e-sign provider, Cybersecurity 22 freeze API, People 43 roster** — fail-closed stand-ins
  (`LEGAL_COUNSEL_CHANNEL`, `LEGAL_ESIGN_PROVIDER` refuse to start if set). Envelopes return `created: false`.
- **Applying a §512(i) repeat-infringer policy** — its terms are counsel's (CQ-22); Legal returns counts only.
- **The §512(g)(3) counter-notice element checklist** — not in the research (UNVERIFIED); stored as a hash only.
- **DSAR identity verification and responses** — clocks only (LG-16, secondary source).
- **Hold-notice rendering per matter** — the notice references the approved template; no per-matter variables.
- **Outbound delivery of any text** (DMCA notices, hold notices, obligation alerts) — no delivery channel exists.
- **Polling the e-sign provider** (`status`, `certificate`) — only the gateway event route is built.

## Known limitations

- One process, one lock; state is rebuilt from the log at start.
- The advice guard is a pattern list. It stops the listed forms and their obfuscations (tests: 14 adversarial
  phrasings through a live route, every pattern example, 0 false positives on 1,000 generated benign sentences);
  it will not catch advice phrased outside the list. The primary control is structural: no route returns free text.
- Clause matching is exact after normalization; typographic variants (curly vs straight quotes) are `unmatched`
  and go to counsel.
- Per-client instances of a template share its `doc_id` (e.g. `client_msa`), so `current_version(client_msa)` is the
  highest in-force version, which may be one client's instance. A per-client document-id scheme would need a spec
  change.
- A Compliance-owned counsel row is confirmed at most once per day (the delivery job), after Andre approves it at
  Compliance.
- Retention month/year are 30/365-day approximations; content-identical blobs keep the first writer's subject refs.
- Every 90 days the engagement letter needs a new approved version or memo intake stops (choice 13).
- **Today's compliance-py refuses the spec's `legal37://memos/<id>` evidence URL** (checked by calling its
  `build_proposal`: "source_url must be an http(s) or urn: URL"), so against the real Compliance a memo's proposal
  ends `refused_by_compliance`. The live run used a Compliance stub. `urn:legal37:memos:<id>` passes Compliance's
  current check; changing the format is a spec decision, not taken here.

## Unlock list — what must exist before a real document is approved or a real memo flips a Compliance row

1. Andre approves the Legal rules seed (LG-00).
2. Counsel engaged (LR decision 1): an engagement letter with the AI-use clause `ENG-AI-01` (CQ-24) is uploaded by
   Andre, counsel's countersigned copy recorded, Andre approves -> a `counsel_ref`. Until a counsel channel adapter
   exists, Andre carries the packages to counsel himself.
3. Per document: counsel's draft uploaded by Andre; a counsel memo citing `doc_id@version` filed; the sign-off
   recorded on the exact hash; Andre approves. `ic_agreement` also needs a CQ-17 memo; `not_legal_advice_v1` a
   CQ-15 memo. Template fills need their own sign-off on the filled hash.
4. Clickwrap acceptances become evidence-sufficient only after a CQ-19 memo; clipper agreements also need Clipper
   Network to capture the ESIGN consent block (`LEGAL_ESIGN_CONSENT_REQUIRED=1`).
5. Obligations need an approved playbook for the document type (a memo citing its clauses) and an
   evidence-sufficient acceptance.
6. For a memo to flip a Compliance row: compliance-py accepts `legal37://memos/<id>` (or the spec changes the
   format), accepts counsel-row `supersede` only from `legal_37` (L4); Legal is configured with
   `LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_URL` / `_TOKEN` / `_CALLER_TOKEN` (Compliance's `legal_37` caller token); Andre types the
   replacement row from the memo (guidance, primary, verified, `source_url` = the memo URL, `verified_at` = the
   receipt date) and then approves it at Compliance; Legal's next proposal-delivery run reads it verified.
7. Music: a CQ-21 memo; other platforms only after their library rule is verified and Andre amends LG-12.
8. Deletion by Legal: a CQ-26 memo with periods per retention class.
9. Trademark filings `ready`: a CQ-27 memo.
10. Holds that actually freeze and notify: Cybersecurity 22's freeze API, People 43's roster and acknowledgments, an
    approved `lit_hold_notice` template.
11. E-sign envelopes: a provider adapter (LR decision 7) and the `esign_gateway` caller.
12. The seven caller changes in the spec, so other departments read Legal instead of their stand-ins.
13. Counsel confirms the business-day definition and the §512(g)(3) checklist; the holiday list is extended past
    2030.

## Amendment — AEGIS round 17 (fix 18, Sep 27, 2026)

Branch `fix18-fin-legal` from `integration-2026-09-24` @ `680c289`. Tests: `tests/test_aegis_r17.py` (64) and
`tests/contract_compliance_real.py`; existing tests that enshrined the flaws were changed (report lists them).

1. **Acceptance binds to the party's instance (N17-4).** A scheduler fill must name `party_ref`; the version records
   it (and `unresolved_fields`, true when any `{{...}}` is left). An acceptance (and an envelope) is refused 409
   `TEMPLATE_NOT_ACCEPTABLE` for a template (declared variables or unresolved fields) and `INSTANCE_NOT_BOUND` for an
   instance bound to another party, or a fill recorded before this fix with no party. A version bound to no party
   with no placeholder is a standard form (e.g. the clipper agreement) and any party may accept it -- Andre's
   explicit choice when he uploads one text for everyone. `clickwrap_sufficient` takes the binding as a required
   input, so evidence is never sufficient for an unbound instance on any path.
2. **Server-assigned version numbers (N17-5).** `DocVersionCreate` has no `version`: Legal assigns the next number,
   monotonic per document and never reused (withdrawn numbers included) -- `1.0`, then the next minor; Andre may ask
   for the next major (`bump: major`). Only Andre's token uploads text; any other caller doing so gets 403 (fills
   stay the scheduler's). A caller can no longer freeze a document by burning `9999.9999`.
3. **IP addresses (N17-6).** Every string (and key) of every write body and every path id is scanned
   (`textguard.ip_in`): IPv4 dotted (leading zeros, a port, embedded in a ref such as `client:192.168.1.1`), the
   `ip-10-0-0-1` host form, IPv6 full/compressed/IPv4-mapped/zone, each also after percent-decoding -> 422
   `IP_ADDRESS_REFUSED`. Candidates are confirmed by the `ipaddress` module (timestamps and 2-part versions never
   hit). Integer (`3232235777`) and hex (`0a000001`) encodings are NOT detected: indistinguishable from counts and
   hashes. The same scan was swept into finance-py.
4. **Advice: structure first (N17-7).** The control is structural: every document version, fills and SOWs
   included, becomes current only after counsel's sign-off on its exact hash (the SOW exception is removed:
   `documents.json` `sow.counsel_required: true`); document text leaves Legal only through the blob store by hash;
   answers to non-Andre callers carry ids, enums, dates, amounts, hashes and Legal's own reason lines, never caller
   free text (the sign-off `topic` is now an id; a test drives a marker phrase through the free-text inputs of the
   non-Andre routes and checks no answer echoes it). The phrase guard stays as defense in depth, extended with
   AP-15..AP-22 (Spanish, German/French/Portuguese, Chinese, conditional, recommendation, imperative, third-person,
   assurance phrasings) and a normalizer that keeps letters of every script; the round-17 list goes from 30 missed
   of 38 to 0 of 38, and the 1,000-sentence benign set still has 0 false positives. Pinned seed hashes:
   `documents.json` `c4819ab8017e4d563a776b0e57964ff821b69f000ec5189581be858fb933f823`, `advice_patterns.json`
   `cf43405ee79ac0779ac466605b31e7ea6e61fa9ba9a7af1ca3e7a440a1719be3`.
5. **Memo -> Compliance proposal (N17-8).** Two steps: `POST /legal/v1/memos` files the memo (answers carry no
   rows; a Compliance-owned question it answers is `awaiting_proposal`), then
   `POST /legal/v1/memos/{memo_id}/compliance-proposals` (Andre) with rows whose `source_url` must equal
   `urn:legal37:memos:<memo_id>`; evidence uses the same urn (compliance-py refused the old `legal37://` URL). Every
   target must be cited by the memo (a `verified_rule` answer for supersede; `cites.obligation_ids` for amend /
   reverify). `tests/contract_compliance_real.py` proves the path in one process against the REAL compliance-py
   app: delivered (201, real proposal id) -> Andre approves at Compliance -> Legal's delivery job reads the row back
   and only then marks CQ-11 verified.
6. **Blob subject refs grow (N17-14).** A blob's `subject_refs` and retention `classes` are sets that grow with
   every writer of the same bytes (within one operation and across operations; no cap that could drop a held
   subject); `last_ref_at` restarts the retention clock; retention deletes only when every class is verified and
   past its period and no active hold covers any of the refs.
7. **Port answers (N17-3 sweep).** `src/answers.py`: strict pydantic checks of `Delivery`, `ProposalAnswer`,
   `ComplianceRow` and `EnvelopeAnswer` in `Op.call`, the proposal delivery and the row confirmation. A malformed
   answer is refused whole (`adapter_answer_refused`) and the fail-closed fallback is used -- a malformed
   `create_proposal` answer used to be recorded as `delivered` with a non-string id.
